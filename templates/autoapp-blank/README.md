# __APP_NAME__

__APP_DESCRIPTION__

A blank Broapp application: one page, no routes, no data. Open the launcher,
select it, and tell the engineer what it should do — it edits this workspace,
builds a candidate release, and shows you a preview before anything replaces
what you are running. `PRODUCT.md` is where you answer who uses this and what
they come to do; the engineer reads it before it writes a word of your
interface, and it cannot change it.

An application runs as trusted local code: crash isolated from the launcher,
not permission isolated from you.
