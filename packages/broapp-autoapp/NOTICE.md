## Impeccable

The `design` reference topic — `src/engineer/design.ts`, served by the
`spec.reference` tool as the topic `design` — is distilled from the **Impeccable**
design skill by **Paul Bakaus**, licensed under the
**Apache License, Version 2.0**.

- Source: <https://github.com/paulbakaus/impeccable> · <https://impeccable.style>
- Licence: <https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0>

**This is a modification.** The text was rewritten, not copied. Impeccable is
written for people and agents who write CSS and components; this renderer draws a
declarative specification with six component kinds and no generated browser code,
so every line was restated to name one of those kinds or a property the view
types declare, and everything the renderer cannot express was left out — layout,
type pairing, timing, the brand register, and the skill's live mode. The material
drawn on is its product register, its copy, cognitive-load, resilience,
first-run and contrast references, and the shared design laws in its `SKILL.md`.
